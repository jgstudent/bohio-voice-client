# SDK Vendor Inquiry Drafts

These drafts contain no confidential project data. Review them before sending
from an authorized Bohio Systems business address. Do not commit replies,
quotes, agreements, personal contact details, or vendor portal credentials.

## Inquiry A — Belledonne Communications / Liblinphone

Subject: Proprietary Liblinphone license inquiry for five-platform business softphone

Hello,

Bohio Systems is evaluating Liblinphone for a closed-source, Bohio-branded
business audio softphone. The planned client targets Windows, macOS, Linux,
Android, and iOS and interoperates with standards-based SIP infrastructure.

Version 1 requires SIP registration, incoming and outgoing audio calls, DTMF,
mute, hold/resume, blind and attended transfer, audio-device selection,
TLS/SRTP, NAT traversal, mobile background calling, push integration, Android
Telecom/Core-Telecom integration, and iOS CallKit/PushKit integration. Video,
conferencing, messaging, and recording are outside the initial scope.

Please provide written information covering:

- proprietary and white-label rights for all five platforms;
- App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store, direct desktop, enterprise, QA, and
  laboratory distribution;
- permitted employees, contractors, CI systems, build agents, artifact stores,
  symbols, and disaster-recovery copies;
- binary versus source delivery and supported wrappers for each platform;
- required SDK version, supported OS versions, maintenance cadence, security
  fixes, end-of-life policy, and upgrade rights;
- support plans, response targets, professional services, and escalation;
- mobile push and native call-integration support and any required server-side
  components;
- included and excluded third-party components, codecs, patents, export
  declarations, attributions, and additional licenses;
- use of the Bohio brand without Linphone branding; and
- pricing, payment, renewal, usage or seat limits, and termination terms.

Please also confirm whether a single agreement can cover one application family
and all listed distribution channels.

Thank you.

## Reply handling checklist

- Verify the sender and vendor domain before opening attachments.
- Do not provide SIP credentials, private network diagrams, customer data, or
  administrator access.
- Store quotes and agreements outside Git with restricted business access.
- Record quote date, validity period, currencies, taxes, renewal, and support
  separately.
- Escalate contract interpretation to qualified counsel; repository documents
  are engineering controls, not legal advice.
