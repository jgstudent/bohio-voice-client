# Version 1 Scope

## Included

- secure device enrollment and account removal;
- SIP registration state and actionable connection errors;
- incoming and outgoing audio calls;
- answer, reject, hang up, mute, hold, resume, and DTMF;
- blind and attended transfer after interoperability validation;
- caller identity presentation subject to privacy policy;
- local contacts integration with explicit permission;
- bounded call history and customer-controlled deletion;
- microphone, speaker, wired headset, and Bluetooth selection;
- light, dark, high-contrast, large-text, keyboard, and screen-reader support;
- safe diagnostic export with secret and personal-data redaction; and
- signed application packages and an approved update path.

## Deferred beyond Version 1

- video calls and conferencing;
- meetings and screen sharing;
- team messaging, SMS, and MMS;
- call recording and transcription;
- CRM and help-desk integrations;
- contact-center supervision and analytics;
- AI summaries, coaching, or sentiment analysis; and
- a browser softphone.

## Acceptance principles

Calling reliability, audio routing, security, privacy, accessibility, battery
behavior, and recoverability take precedence over feature count. A platform is
not accepted because it compiles; it must pass real-device calling, network
transition, suspend/resume, permission, update, and revocation tests.
